# Stacked Delight (v0.4)

A fast-food add-on for **Farmer's Delight** and **Create** (NeoForge 1.21.1).

## Build it
1. Upload the CONTENTS of this folder to your GitHub repo (also the hidden `.github` folder).
2. Open the **Actions** tab, wait for the green check.
3. Download **stacked-delight-jar** and put the .jar in your `mods` folder, next to Farmer's Delight.

## The menu (all recipes are shapeless unless noted)
| Item | Recipe | Food |
|---|---|---|
| Burger Bun | Bake Create Dough (furnace, smoker, campfire or Encased Fan with smoke) | 4 hunger |
| Triple Stacked Burger | 3 FD Hamburgers in a column (shaped) | 20 hunger, Nourishment + Comfort |
| Large Fries | 3 Baked Potatoes + FD Tomato Sauce + Paper | 4 portions x 4 hunger |
| 20 Piece Chicken Nuggets | 4 FD Cooked Chicken Cuts + FD Wheat Dough + Paper | 20 pieces x 2 hunger |
| Chicken Burger | 2 Burger Buns + FD Cooked Chicken Cuts + FD Cabbage Leaf + FD Tomato | 13 hunger, Nourishment |
| Bacon Double Burger | 2 Burger Buns + 2 FD Beef Patties + 2 FD Cooked Bacon | 17 hunger, Nourishment |
| Onion Rings | 2 FD Onions + FD Wheat Dough + Paper | 6 rings x 2 hunger |
| Chocolate Milkshake | **Create Mixer**: FD Milk Bottle + 2 Cocoa Beans + Sugar | 6 hunger, Comfort, gives the bottle back |
| Sweet Berry Milkshake | **Create Mixer**: FD Milk Bottle + 2 Sweet Berries + Sugar | 6 hunger, Comfort, gives the bottle back |
| Combo Meal | Triple Burger + Large Fries + Nuggets + a Milkshake | 20 hunger, Absorption II (5 min), Nourishment (10 min), can be eaten when full |

Fries, nuggets and rings are eaten one piece at a time; the bar shows what is left.
