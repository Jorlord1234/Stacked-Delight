# Stacked Delight (v0.5)

A fast-food add-on for **Farmer's Delight** and **Create** (NeoForge 1.21.1).

## Build it
1. Upload the CONTENTS of this folder to your GitHub repo (also the hidden `.github` folder).
2. Open the **Actions** tab, wait for the green check.
3. Download **stacked-delight-jar** and put the .jar in your `mods` folder.

## Machines (right-click with items; empty hand shows the tanks; shift + empty hand empties it)
**Ice Cream Machine**: Milk Bottle (+4) or Milk Bucket (+12) fills the milk mix. Sugar = vanilla, Cocoa Beans = chocolate,
Sweet Berries = strawberry (+4 each). Right-click with a Wafer Cone to get a cone. Switching flavor empties the flavor tank.

**Soda Machine**: Water Bucket (+8) fills the fizzy water. Cocoa Beans = Cola, FD Apple Cider = Apple, FD Melon Juice = Melon,
Sweet Berries = Berry. Right-click with a Soda Cup to get a soda; drinking gives the cup back.
Sodas: Cola = Speed, Apple = Jump Boost, Melon = Haste, Berry = Night Vision.

Recipes: Ice Cream Machine = 7 Iron + Blue Ice + Create Andesite Casing. Soda Machine = 7 Iron + Glass + Andesite Casing. Soda Cup = 3 Paper -> 2.
